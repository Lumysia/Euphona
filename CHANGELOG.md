# Changelog

What changes in each version, for people using the app.

## 1.0.0

The first version of Euphona for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

- **Voice analysis:** read a passage, or import an audio or video file, and get an overall score from
  timbre, pitch, flow and ease, the feminine/masculine balance, and what has the most to gain. Replay
  any recording and see how each moment scored.
- **Coach:** a ten-minute lesson a day that places you from your analyses and adapts to every try;
  tell it a lesson is too easy, too hard, or that your voice is tired, or talk to it.
- **Practice:** live pitch with a target range, live timbre, resonance, a live spectrum, a piano for
  reference notes, and a voice quality check.
- **Progress:** trends over 7, 30 or 90 days or all time, a practice calendar, a daily streak, your full
  history, and export of an analysis or the whole list as an image or a PDF.
- Euphona's own models, trained on public recordings in 53 languages; everything runs on your device.
- History syncs with iCloud; six theme palettes; light and dark mode.
- Available in 34 languages.
