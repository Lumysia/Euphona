# Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

Euphona is built so that your voice stays yours. This policy explains what the app does with your
information on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## In short

- Every analysis runs on your device. Your recordings are never sent to us or to any server to be
  scored.
- There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- The app sends nothing to us. Feedback is something you post yourself on GitHub.

## What stays on your device

- **Recordings and results.** When you analyze your voice, the recording, its results and your
  progress are stored on your device. Euphona's own models analyze them there.
- **Coach.** Lessons, your practice tries, your check-ins and your conversation with the coach are
  stored on your device. The coach's language model is Apple Intelligence on your device; Euphona does
  not send your conversation to us.
- **Speech transcription.** When a lesson asks you to speak freely, the words are transcribed on your
  device with Apple's speech recognition, only to give you feedback on that try.
- **Settings**, such as your voice goal and theme.

## iCloud

If iCloud sync is on (you choose this at the start and can change it in Settings), your history and
recordings are stored in your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices.
That data is under your Apple Account and Apple's iCloud terms; we cannot read it.

## Feedback you send

**Settings → Send Feedback** helps you write a report and then opens GitHub, where you post it as an
issue from your own GitHub account. The app itself sends nothing. Before you continue, it shows you
what it adds to your post if you leave diagnostics on: the app and model version, your device model,
system version and language, and your last five overall scores. It never adds a recording or your
voice goal.

Issues on GitHub are public and are handled under GitHub's terms. You can edit or delete your own
issue there at any time.

## Microphone

Euphona asks for the microphone only to record your voice for analysis and practice. It listens only
while a recording or a live tool is running.

## Deleting your data

**Settings → Erase All Data** deletes everything Euphona stored, on this device and in your iCloud.
Deleting the app removes what is on the device.

## Children

Euphona is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

## Not medical advice

Euphona's measures, including Voice Quality and Ease, are estimates of how a voice sounds. They are
not a diagnosis. If speaking hurts or hoarseness lasts, see a doctor or a speech-language therapist.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date. Questions:
[open an issue](https://github.com/Lumysia/Euphona/issues), or use Send Feedback in the app.
